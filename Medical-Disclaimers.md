KinaBot is provided for informational, educational, and personal reflection purposes only.

KinaBot is not intended, designed, or validated to diagnose, prevent, monitor, treat, or cure any disease or medical condition. It is not a medical device and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care, clinical cognitive assessment, therapy, or emergency support.

Speech and language patterns can be influenced by many factors, including environment, stress, fatigue, language background, microphone quality, mood, hearing, accent, medication, sleep, and the topic being discussed. KinaBot's analysis should be treated as a limited communication-pattern summary, not a clinical conclusion.

Users should always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical concerns. Do not disregard, delay, or avoid professional medical advice because of information generated by KinaBot.

KinaBot does not provide emergency services. If you believe someone may be in immediate danger or experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services.

Any use of KinaBot with older adults, family members, patients, caregivers, or vulnerable individuals should be based on clear consent, respect, and human oversight.
