3) What is KinaBot?

KinaBot is a digital health software tool that turns everyday conversation into actionable trend insights:

Users upload short voice recordings or conversation audio

The system extracts acoustic + linguistic features

It calculates key indicators and generates a readable score and trend view over time (baseline vs. follow-ups)

Outputs are delivered as an easy-to-share web report / dashboard (and optional PDF export)

Important Disclaimer: KinaBot is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. It provides informational insights to support awareness and encourage next-step action


4) Why It Matters

Today, most cognitive assessments are either clinical, time-consuming, or require specialized access. KinaBot aims to make cognitive trend awareness more accessible by leveraging a signal that is:

low-cost (speech is naturally produced every day)

repeatable (trend tracking over time)

device-light (no dedicated hardware required)

easy to integrate into existing platforms (robots, voice assistants, care services)
